## [Unreleased]

- Game modes: Year auction (everyone guesses a year, closest gets the card), Intro sprint (buzz in on growing clips, faster = more tokens, wrong = out for that song) and a Generation war switch (teams by birth year, each gets the other generation's songs).
- Rooms: play with everyone on their own phone. The host opens a room (QR code + four-letter code), players join from their phones and play their own turns and challenges; the host device runs the game, the music and the settings.
- Swedish/English switch, house rule "right spot + named = go again", player order, about 440 more non-rock songs.
